## What happened

On 22 October 2026, a US appeals court overturned an order blocking a Mississippi education law and directed that a different judge take over the case.

This is a new consequence of an older mistake. The original order, issued on 20 July 2025, contained invented facts and people. The appeals opinion says Judge Henry Wingate acknowledged AI use and attributed drafting errors to his clerk's use of it.

## Why it went wrong

Unsupported material reached a court order. Even a corrected version cited a case that does not exist, according to the appeals court.

## Impact

The case returns to a different judge. The appeal also found separate legal errors, so AI-written mistakes were not the only reason for overturning the order.

No AI product or maker was identified.

## The lesson

Every fact and citation needs checking against the case record. Correcting some errors is not enough if the rest remain unchecked.
