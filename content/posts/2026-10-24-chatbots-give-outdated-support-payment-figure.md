## What happened

A Full Fact study covered on 15 October 2026 found chatbots repeating an outdated UK support-payment amount.

In some answers, Claude and Gemini 3.1 Pro gave £8.86 a week. Full Fact says that rate had risen to £9.95 in June 2025. This is the rate for asylum seekers whose accommodation provides meals.

## Why it went wrong

An old figure survived in answers that were otherwise largely accurate. Some tools presented both amounts without explaining which applied.

## Impact

Someone relying on those replies could misunderstand the support available. The research does not establish that anyone lost money because of them.

Full Fact's July–September checks found many correct answers too. The examples do not measure how often every chatbot answer is wrong.

## The lesson

Check payment figures against the current official page, including the date and who the rate applies to. A confident answer can still contain an old number.
