## What happened

NewsGuard tests reported on 21 October 2026 found Google's AI Overview and Gemini, and Microsoft's Copilot, repeating a false accusation about Texas Senate candidate James Talarico.

A fake video impersonated a former student. PolitiFact reported that the real man denied making the accusation.

## Why it went wrong

The tools treated the supposed accusation as real. According to WindowsForum's account of NewsGuard's tests, Google's summary linked to two genuine news articles that did not mention it.

Those links made the answer look supported without supporting the claim.

## Impact

A fabricated accusation reached AI answers during an election campaign. The reports do not establish how many users saw those answers or whether they changed anyone's vote.

## The lesson

Open the sources, especially for serious accusations about people. A real news link does not guarantee that an AI's claim appears in the article.
